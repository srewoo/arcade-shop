## Extra bugs found and fixed

- `P` (and `M`) were ignored whenever a button had focus, so P could not close the pause overlay (focus is on Resume). Letters now only ignored in select/input.
- The STRIKE banner and callout survived a restart (they kept their 1.6 s timers); `clearBanners()` on start.
- The Lock button read "Rolling..." on the game-over screen; now "Game over".
- CSS cascade: the landscape block was overridden by the later 700 px controls rule (buttons wrapped); landscape rules now load last.
- "Drag up to throw" pill sat over the pocket label in landscape; moved to the top-right of the stage.
- High-contrast guide was invisible on the light wood (yellow on tan); added a dark underlay, full opacity and outlined labels.
- Sizing note: a depth factor of 0.5 alone (the report's number) separated rack rows by about 2 px, hence the rack-only screen lift.

## Known remaining issues

- Real audio not auditioned; mix balance, crowd burst and limiter threshold are by code and fake-context tests only.
- Interpolation not seen on a real 120/144 Hz display; headless runs 60 Hz. Real-device touch feel, iOS/safe-area notch handling and screen readers (VoiceOver/NVDA) not run.
- Colour-blind check used a matrix filter, not a validated simulator.
- Item 01's literal "8% of the aim x spin grid" target is not met (3.2%, assist 4.9%); casual-player strike rate is 10.8% (assist 15.6%). Balance numbers come from scripted bots, not people.
- The pocket call uses where the ball crosses the head-pin line (within 3.5 cm of +/-9 cm), not a literal 0.005 rad band.
- The rack separation is a screen-space lift (no real camera pitch); the back wall and sign sit a little higher and the sign is partly clipped at the top of short stages.
- Blur auto-pause also fires when the user clicks into another window or devtools (by convention); resume is manual.
- Title and results dialogs ignore Esc by design. At 150% text on a 360 px phone the Settings dialog scrolls inside itself.
- The lane layer cache only helps while the camera is still (aiming); during the roll the lane is redrawn each frame (still 60 fps at 4x throttle).
- Large text does not scale the in-canvas "LUCKY LANES" sign. Spare-challenge games are not counted in the lifetime strike/spare stats.
- Hotseat/spare multi-human CPU modes were exercised through scripted full games only, not by hand.
