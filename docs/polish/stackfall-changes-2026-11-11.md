## Extra bugs found and fixed

- Touch pad: a tap shorter than one 16.7 ms tick on Left/Right/Down was lost (also in the original). `Controls.setVirtual` now latches the press (found with a Playwright tap; test moved the piece 3 -> 2 -> 4 as expected afterwards).
- Zen: a blocked stash spawn set `status='over'` even in Zen (covered by the STACKFALL-01 fix).
- Own regression caught during verification: `Ambient.stop()` created an `AudioContext` at page load (autoplay warning); it now never creates one.
- Own regression caught: hiding the HUD on the small-phone title made the CSS grid auto-place the stage into an `auto` row (collapsed to 24 px); rows are now explicit.
- Stacked banners overlapped when the first had a sub-line ("Quad / Combo x1" over "Level 4"); spacing is now cumulative.
- The old clear flash used pre-collapse row positions over the post-collapse board; replaced by the dissolve animation.
- Top-bar chips are inert while a card is open (focus trap); the title card has its own Settings button and `M` still mutes.

## Known remaining issues

- Real audio output, VoiceOver/NVDA, real-device touch feel, 120/144 Hz smoothness and low-end phone performance were not testable in headless Chromium.
- `tuned` difficulty/scoring preset is available but not the default (STACKFALL-19/26): needs a human playtest and an owner decision on score-ladder fidelity.
- First interaction being Play still shows one ~156 ms task in headless Chromium (AudioContext construction).
- Touch niceties from STACKFALL-27 not changed: short slow drags do nothing, soft drop still needs the finger held below the start point.
- First-run help strip makes the title card scroll on 320x568 (single scroll region, heading and selected mode visible first).
- No automated axe-core run and no CI max-lines rule (no new dependencies, no CI in the folder).
- Default palette still has the original small-delta pairs under protan/deutan/tritan (sapphire/amethyst 6.7, rose/jade 8.5, copper/rose 13.6); colour-blind assist and the per-shape glyphs are the remedy.
- Mix levels were measured offline from the synth graph, not by ear.
