    How to play

    • ← → or A D: slide along the foul line. Line up with a pocket tick (the two white marks).
    • Space / Enter: lock position, aim, power, then hook (the last lock throws). Once the pins settle it skips the replay.
    • Backspace: step back one meter. Esc / P: pause. M: mute.
    • Mouse or touch: drag up from anywhere on the lane to throw. Longer drag = more power, a curved drag = hook. Tap the lane to lock the current meter.
    • Aim for the pocket, just beside the head pin, with good power to make strikes. The tinted centre of the aim bar is the sweet spot.
